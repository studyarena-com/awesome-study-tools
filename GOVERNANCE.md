# Governance

Awesome Study Tools is stewarded by the `studyarena-com` GitHub organization for the benefit of its contributors and users.

## Decision making

Routine corrections are accepted when they satisfy the published editorial policy and validation checks. Material changes—new categories, scope changes, affiliated listings, disputed removals, or governance changes—should be discussed in an issue before merging.

Maintainers aim for evidence-based consensus. When consensus is not possible, a repository maintainer records the decision and rationale publicly. Decisions may be revisited when better evidence appears.

## Maintainers

Maintainers review contributions, keep automation healthy, manage releases, and apply the conflict-of-interest rules. Contributors who consistently provide careful reviews and regionally informed improvements may be invited to maintain the project.

A maintainer should not be the sole substantive reviewer of a change that benefits a product with which they are materially affiliated when another qualified reviewer is available.

## Releases and data stability

The `main` branch contains the current catalog. Tagged releases provide stable snapshots for downstream users. Breaking data-schema changes require a schema-version increment and release note; additive catalog changes do not.

## Policy changes

Changes to `EDITORIAL_POLICY.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, or the license require a focused pull request that explains the impact. The CC0 dedication already applied to prior contributions cannot be revoked retroactively.
