# Code of conduct

We want this project to be useful to learners everywhere. Participation should be welcoming, patient, and grounded in evidence.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful across differences in language, location, education system, identity, and experience.
- Critique claims and changes, not people.
- Share regional knowledge without treating one market or curriculum as the default.
- Disclose relevant product affiliations and avoid promotional behaviour.
- Protect learner privacy and never post personal student data.
- Accept corrections gracefully and help make decisions understandable to newcomers.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate misinformation, spam, and undisclosed paid promotion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/studyarena-com/awesome-study-tools/security/advisories/new). Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as possible.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
